The Problem

A $18B+ Market With Zero Real Innovation

Despite the personal care accessories market exceeding $18 billion globally, the fundamental design of bathing tools has seen virtually no meaningful innovation in decades. Traditional bathing implements all suffer from the same core structural limitations.

$18B+ The global personal care accessories market is enormous, growing, and profoundly underserved by meaningful product innovation. The last category-defining bathing product was the loofah — and it has not meaningfully changed in over a century.
01
Interruption of Routine

Users must stop bathing to reach for and apply soap, disrupting the shower experience, reducing efficiency, and creating significant inconvenience — particularly for individuals with limited mobility.

02
Single-Surface Limitation

Loofahs, washcloths, and sponges offer only one texture and one function. Consumers must own multiple tools to exfoliate, cleanse, and perform sensitive skin care — cluttering the shower and increasing cost.

03
Hygiene Degradation

Traditional tools accumulate bacteria rapidly due to moisture retention, lack of ventilation, and improper storage — creating a genuine hygiene risk that consumers are increasingly aware of and concerned about.

The Solution

One Mitt. Three Surfaces. Zero Interruptions.

Patent PendingDesign & Utility

No existing product — from any brand — offers all of these features in combination. VersaLux is not competing in a category. It is creating one.

🔄

Three Usable Surfaces

Exfoliating outer surface (closed), terry cloth front (reversed), terry cloth back (face-safe) — one mitt, three complete functions.

💧

Integrated Soap Delivery System

Squeeze-bulb dispenser with check valve in a Velcro-sealed palm pouch — soap delivered directly to the cleaning surface with no reaching, no interruptions.

🥈

Dual-Mode Magnetic Closure

Neodymium magnets create a secure seal in both closed and reversed positions — smooth, silent, single-handed operation every time.

📚

Book-Spine Assembly

Permanently sewn left seam; magnetic right seam enables smooth single-handed reversal between modes with no fumbling or removal.

🤝

Ambidextrous Design

Fitted wrist cuff, lateral-edge hanging loop, and dual thumb holes — comfortable for left- and right-handed users of all ages.

👪

Multiple Sizing

Available in Adult Large, Medium, Small, and Junior — one consistent premium experience designed for the whole family.

Our Story

Built from a decade of conviction and one relentless founder.

Robert Herron Jr. founded VersaLux Innovations LLC in 2016 in Cibolo, Texas, with one goal: to eliminate the unnecessary complexity of the everyday shower routine.

What started as a simple question — "Why does a simple shower require so many separate products?" — became a design challenge, then a working prototype, and now a patent-pending product category of its own.

VersaLux is a pre-revenue consumer goods startup developing the world's first premium bathing mitt with an integrated soap dispensing system. A decade of founder commitment has produced a trademarked brand, a patent-pending product, and a clear path to commercial launch.
