The Problem

Your Shower Is Overloaded.

The average shower holds 7–12 separate products — loofahs, sponges, washcloths, scrubbers, multiple soap bottles. Every one of them is something extra to buy, store, clean, and replace. VersaLux Body Mate was designed to change that.

01
Too Many Products

Loofahs, sponges, washcloths, exfoliating gloves — each does one thing. A complete routine requires all of them.

02
Constant Interruption

Reaching for soap, switching tools, fumbling mid-shower — the routine is broken up constantly by product limitations.

03
Fast Hygiene Decline

Loofahs and sponges trap bacteria within days. Most people use them far longer than they should — a genuine hygiene risk.

What VersaLux Body Mate Brings Together

Different Surfaces. One Product.

Each surface and component of VersaLux Body Mate serves a specific purpose — giving you a complete bathing experience without reaching for anything else.

01
Terry-Microfiber Blend

Premium combined fabric — terry cloth softness for everyday cleansing, microfiber performance for gentle facial and sensitive skin care, woven into one layer.

02
Silicone Exfoliating

A textured silicone surface built into the construction — deep exfoliation and circulation without a separate scrubber.

03
Nylon Outer Layer

The exfoliating nylon exterior in closed mode — firm, effective cleansing with no extra accessories needed.

04
Built-In Components

Neodymium magnets, internal soap bladder with check valve, fitted wrist cuff, and hanging loop — built into one seamless product.

How the Soap System Works

A Lux Clean in Every Squeeze.

Soap is already where you're washing. No reaching, no interruptions.

Step 01

Fill the Internal Bladder

Fill the squeeze-bulb dispenser with your liquid soap. The check valve keeps it sealed and ready until you need it.

Step 02

Squeeze to Dispense

A gentle squeeze delivers soap directly through the palm pouch to the surface you're washing with — right where you need it.

Step 03

Lather and Continue

No reaching. No interruption. Lather and continue on whichever surface you chose. One product — start to finish.

Key Innovations

One Mitt. Three Surfaces. Zero Interruptions.

No existing product — from any brand — offers all of these features in combination. VersaLux Body Mate is not competing in a category. It is creating one.

🔄

Three Usable Surfaces

Exfoliating outer (closed), terry-microfiber blend front (reversed), gentle back (face-safe) — one mitt, three complete functions.

💧

Integrated Soap Delivery

Squeeze-bulb dispenser with check valve in a Velcro-sealed palm pouch — soap delivered with no reaching.

🥈

Dual-Mode Magnetic Closure

Neodymium magnets secure the mitt in both closed and reversed positions. Single-handed operation.

📚

Book-Spine Assembly

Permanently sewn left seam; magnetic right seam enables smooth single-handed reversal between modes.

🤝

Ambidextrous Design

Fitted wrist cuff, hanging loop, dual thumb holes — comfortable for left- and right-handed users.

👪

Multiple Sizing

Adult Large, Medium, Small, and Junior — one premium experience for the whole family.

Our Story

Built from a decade of conviction and one relentless founder.

Robert Herron Jr. founded VersaLux Innovations LLC in 2016 in Cibolo, Texas, with one goal: eliminate the unnecessary complexity of the everyday shower routine.

What started as a simple question — "Why does a simple shower require so many separate products?" — became a design challenge, then a working prototype, and now a patent-pending product in a category of its own.

A decade of commitment has produced a trademarked brand, a patent-pending product, and a clear path to commercial launch.
